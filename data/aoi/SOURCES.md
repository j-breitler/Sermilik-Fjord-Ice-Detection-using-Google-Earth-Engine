# Data Sources — AOI

## Sermilik_Fjord_Boundary.geojson

Converted from the original shapefile distributed with:

> Moon, T. A., Cohen, B., Black, T. E., Laidre, K. L., Stern, H. L., and Joughin, I.:
> Characterizing southeast Greenland fjord surface ice and freshwater flux to support
> biological applications, *The Cryosphere*, 18, 4845–4872,
> https://doi.org/10.5194/tc-18-4845-2024, 2024.

Dataset available at: https://nsidc.org/data/nsidc-0796/versions/1

**Original CRS:** EPSG:3413 (NSIDC Sea Ice Polar Stereographic North)  
**Converted to:** EPSG:4326 (WGS 84) on import via `src/utils.py`
